**Teams hosted on a Mac work fully with Claude Code again.** Since 0.5.0, Claude Code's hooks couldn't reach a team hosted on someone's Mac, because of the encrypted connection's self-signed certificate. On those teams Claude got no team context, wasn't asked before a conflicting edit or a risky push, and showed as Idle while it worked. Protoplasma now passes Claude's hooks on through your own Mac, pinned to that hub's certificate. Teams on the online hub weren't affected.
