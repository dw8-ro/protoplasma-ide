**Inviting people is easier to find.** The People page now has its own Invite people section.

**If you're on 0.4.x or 0.5.0:** after you click Update now, the window closes but Protoplasma keeps running, so the update doesn't go in. Force Quit Protoplasma once (Option-Command-Esc, or right-click it in the Dock with Option held). The update then installs and the app opens on 0.5.6.

What's new:
- People → Invite people: the team owner invites someone by their GitHub username, and is reminded that they'll also need access to the team's repositories on GitHub. Teammates see who can invite.
- Electron 44.10.0, with Chromium's latest security fixes.
