**A quieter look.** Protoplasma keeps its black and white, with less in the way of your agents.

**If you're on 0.4.x or 0.5.0:** after you click Update now, the window closes but Protoplasma keeps running, so the update doesn't go in. Force Quit Protoplasma once (Option-Command-Esc, or right-click it in the Dock with Option held). The update then installs and the app opens on 0.5.7.

What's new:
- The main area sits on the window as one rounded panel, so the title bar and sidebar no longer need dividing lines. Settings and the update bar match it.
- Each agent's terminal is a plain card. The owner's name is inside it, with their colour as a small dot, in place of a coloured outline with the name cut into it.
- Profile pictures are flat circles in each person's colour, without the ring around them.
- Menus are slightly see-through, and text fields show a soft ring while you type in them.
