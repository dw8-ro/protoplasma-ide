Protoplasma visual pass

Simpler and quieter, still black and white. Fewer boxes inside boxes, so the agents' terminals get the room and the only colour left is status. Nothing here is merged or released: pin notes on anything you want changed.

The aim: minimal but sufficient. Someone new can find their way at once, and an experienced engineer still has every tool. The simplifications decide what shows first and put the rest one click away; nothing is taken out.

Start on your own: "Just me" (new)

What changed

  • Someone with no team lands on Sessions straight after signing in. The title bar says "Just me". Their first choice is what the agent should do.
  • It's private: a space on their own Mac that nothing outside sees, until they invite someone.
  • Settings → Team offers the two ways to work with people: invite them here (same Wi-Fi), or make a team (anywhere).
  • If a teammate adds them to a team, an alert says "You were added to Acme". One click opens it.
  • Anyone who already has a team sees no change: the app opens their last team, as before.

Moving work into a team

  • Move to a team… sits in each session's menu and its Session panel.
  • The session is made in the team with the same name and goal. Agents you start there work in the same folder and branch, so the work carries on, changes not yet committed included.
  • Teammates get your work once you Commit & Push; the move reminds you if anything isn't pushed.
  • The old session is marked done. Its plan, notes and comments stay with it.

Settings: Team and Account (new since your first look)

What changed

  • Leave team (or Delete team, for the owner) used to sit right next to Switch team, in the same grey. It now has its own row under its own heading, with what it does written beside a red button.
  • Sign out is red here and in the team picker; Remove on the People page and Delete session in its menu are red too.
  • Each one still asks you to confirm, as before.

Still to decide

  1. The team picker (U2): done another way. Nobody needs a team to start: see "Just me" above. The team window stays as it was, for switching and making teams.
  2. Hand off and Continue: settled. They stay as a word on the bar, so the tool is in sight for everyone.
  3. Session cards. Keep the hairline, or go borderless on a soft fill like the buttons?
