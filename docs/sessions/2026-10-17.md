### 3. 0.5.2: Claude Code's hooks reach teams hosted on a Mac
- The README review found that since 0.5.0, every Claude Code hook to a team hosted on a Mac failed silently, because
  of the hub's self-signed certificate. Those teams had no team context, no ask before a risky push or a conflicting
  edit, and the status stuck at Idle.
- Fixed with a loopback relay (`hookRelay` in `app/teams.js`, `hookTarget` in `app/main.js`, `hookBase` in
  `src/lane.js`). Tested in `test/lan.test.js` and with the real `claude` CLI against a hub using a self-signed
  certificate. Released.
